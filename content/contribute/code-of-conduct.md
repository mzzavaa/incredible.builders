**Purpose.** One clear standard for the online surfaces this project runs. For every contributor, and for the people who enforce it.

**Contains.**
- Scope: repositories, issues, Discord, challenges, and office hours
- Out of scope: in-person and co-hosted events run under the community code of conduct, with its own reporting contacts and appeal route
- Expected behaviour, stated as concrete actions rather than adjectives
- Unacceptable behaviour with specific examples
- Reporting: two named contacts, one of them outside the maintainer group
- What happens after a report, with an indicative timeline
- Enforcement ladder from private warning to permanent removal
- Attribution of the source document this is adapted from

**Primary CTA.** Read it before your first contribution, and report anything that breaks it.
