**Purpose.** Make ownership legible. For contributors who need a decision, and for anyone considering taking on a maintainer role.

**Contains.**
- Table of projects to maintainers with response time expectations
- Decision process: lazy consensus by default, escalation path when it fails
- Path to maintainer: contribution history, nomination, and the trial period
- Maintainer responsibilities and the honest time cost per month
- Stepping down gracefully, including the handover checklist
- Projects currently looking for a co-maintainer

**Primary CTA.** Nominate yourself or someone else for a maintainer slot.
