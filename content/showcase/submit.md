**Purpose.** The submission path for the showcase. For a builder who has something working and wants it listed.

**Contains.**
- Two routes: pull request against data/showcase.yaml, or a short form
- Required fields with an annotated example entry
- What counts as evidence: public repo, hosted demo, or a recorded walkthrough
- Review timeline and who reviews
- Edit and removal policy, including how to take your entry down
- What we will never ask for: private code, credentials, client names without consent

**Primary CTA.** Open a pull request with your entry.
