**Purpose.** The wall of builds. Public proof that this community produces working things, and a place for members to get their work seen.

**Contains.**
- Grid rendered from data/showcase.yaml, filterable by category, stack, and year
- Card fields: build name, builder, one-line summary, stack chips, repo or demo link
- "Built during a challenge" badge linking back to the challenge it came from
- Featured build of the month with a longer write-up
- Builder profile links, kept to a handle and one external link
- Submission call-out with the criteria summary

**Primary CTA.** Submit a build.
**Links to.** /showcase/submit/, /showcase/criteria/, /challenges/
