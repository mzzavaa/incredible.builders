**Purpose.** Live, unrehearsed help. For builders stuck on something a chat thread cannot resolve, and for maintainers to hear where the docs fail.

**Contains.**
- Schedule, duration, and the joining link
- Format: queue-based, fifteen minutes per person, screen sharing encouraged
- How to submit a topic in advance and how the queue is ordered
- What gets covered here versus what belongs in a paid engagement
- Recording policy and where recordings are published
- Archive of past sessions with topic tags

**Primary CTA.** Submit your topic before the next session.
