**Purpose.** Remove legal ambiguity so people can reuse the code confidently, including inside companies. For contributors, adopters, and anyone shipping one of these repos into production.

**Contains.**
- Code licences per repo, with the default and the exceptions listed
- Contributor terms: you keep copyright, you grant us a licence to publish
- Developer certificate of origin and the licence header expected on new files
- Third-party assets and how to check the notices file
- Content licence, attribution line, and trademark terms are published once, on incredible-consulting.org, not restated here
- Who to contact for anything not covered

**Primary CTA.** Check the licence on the repo before you reuse it.
