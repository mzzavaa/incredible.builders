**Purpose.** For user group leads, employers, and educators who want to run a build challenge without inventing the format. Everything is reusable under an open licence.

**Contains.**
- Brief template with the four fields that make or break a challenge
- Timeline options: weekend, two week, four week, with effort estimates
- Judging pack: rubric sheet, scoring form, briefing notes for judges
- Infrastructure notes: sandbox accounts, spend caps, and credential hygiene
- Communications kit: announcement, reminder cadence, results post
- Co-hosting with Incredible: what we provide and what we ask for

**Primary CTA.** Download the brief template and pick a date.
