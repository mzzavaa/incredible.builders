**Purpose.** The rulebook. For participants who want certainty before they invest a weekend, and for judges who need a consistent rubric.

**Contains.**
- Eligibility and team size limits
- Reuse policy: pre-existing code, templates, and generated code
- Submission requirements and the hard deadline mechanics
- Scoring rubric with weights: working software, clarity, originality, reusability
- Judge panel composition and conflict of interest handling
- Prizes, recognition, and intellectual property: entrants keep their work
- Disqualification grounds and the appeal window

**Primary CTA.** Read the rubric before you scope your entry.
