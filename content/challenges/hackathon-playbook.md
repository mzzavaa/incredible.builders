**Purpose.** Practical playbook for participants. For anyone who has started a hackathon strong and finished with a broken demo.

**Contains.**
- Hour zero: scoping down to one demonstrable path
- Stack choice rules: boring where it does not matter, sharp where it does
- The pre-built pieces you are allowed to bring, and what counts as cheating
- Checkpoint schedule with a hard freeze before the demo
- Demo craft: three minutes, one path, no live account creation
- The cut list: what to drop at hour 36 without losing the story
- Post-event: turning a hackathon build into a maintained repo
