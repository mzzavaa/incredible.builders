**Purpose.** The build log stream. Honest, incremental notes from work in progress. The value is the dead ends, the numbers, and the decisions that got reversed.

**Contains.**
- Reverse chronological list with date, author, project tag, and reading time
- Filter by project so a reader can follow one build only
- Format rules summary: what happened, what it cost, what we changed, what is next
- Subscribe options: RSS and the Discord channel that mirrors new entries
- Invitation to publish your own log with a link to the template

**Primary CTA.** Subscribe to the feed, or publish your own log.
