**Purpose.** The house format for build logs, so entries stay comparable and useful. For any contributor about to write their first one.

**Contains.**
- The five headings: context, what we tried, what it cost, what broke, next step
- Length guidance and why we cap entries at roughly 500 words
- Rules on numbers: always give units, always give the date, never round to zero
- What to leave in: failed approaches, abandoned branches, wrong assumptions
- What to leave out: client names without consent, credentials, unreleased customer data
- Copy-paste front matter block and file naming convention
- Review flow: open a pull request, one maintainer reads it, merge

**Primary CTA.** Copy the template and open a pull request with your first entry.
